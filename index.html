Our Love Story

Two hearts, one beautiful beginning.

-She wasn't just everything I had longed for, she was more. She fulfilled every dream I had ever carried about the woman I longed to love, effortlessly ticking every box I never thought one person could. Meeting her didn't feel like chance; it felt like destiny wrapped in grace. 11th February, 2025 is a date forever etched in my heart the day the course of my destiny was set in motion. From that moment, everything changed, and what began as an ordinary day became the beginning of an extraordinary love story.

~Mr. Victor Yusuf Obadiah

-I met him during the time when my life felt unsettled, school was demanding more than I could give, and I was learning how to be alone without feeling empty he came into my life through people I already trusted which made him feel familiar. It started with casual hangouts after a long day, a quiet laughter that filled the gap between conversations. I noticed how easily my attention shifted towards him how I listened when he spoke, how small details about him stayed with me, weeks passed by, but he became part of moments I reflect back on. When things become heavy he's presence made them lighter without realizing, he became part of my routine, days become longer if we don't talk. By the time I admitted what was happening, it already felt permanent. He wasn't just someone in my life anymore, he became part of it. I love you soulmate.

~Miss Namunacha Nana Abbas
